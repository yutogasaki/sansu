# Design and motion contract — v4 candidate

The old v3 compact banner is rejected as an art direction. No v3 commit or push. Existing saved streak/light/ride rules can remain; final visuals must be derived from the selected storyboard rather than inherited banner structure.

## Composition

One physical problem board and a keypad. No separate mascot dashboard. The board's rim supports Pokomoko and the star sockets; the character has visible grounded feet at rest. The main question and input remain on clean ivory. Peak acting uses the upper rim and outer sides, never crossing the next live answer. At 390×844: top utilities around 44px, whole question/play board ends before 510px, full keys and help below. The mascot should be around 100px tall at rest and briefly larger at peaks, using overlap to avoid a new illustration-only band.
