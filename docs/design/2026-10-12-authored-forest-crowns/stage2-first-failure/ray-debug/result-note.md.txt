The original uninstrumented query and the transparent-proxy replay both raised `Ray parity exceeded bounded intersections: role-l14-2743`. This is a reproduced numerical failure, not evidence that the trunk and lobe lack an intersection. No joint or art gate passed.

The next actual ray origin matched the recorded expression exactly.

Actual origin-to-next displacement stayed `[0,0,0]` until the unchanged 128-intersection limit raised the failure. The first direction also had a rounded-zero post-hit step, but its next BVH call returned no hit. Thus a zero step does not inevitably cause repeated hits for every surface/direction; the complete cycle was observed for the second direction here.
