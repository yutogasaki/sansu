The purpose is a bright garden interior framed by a rear canopy and two unequal side returns. The former trials put crown volumes in a visible row. Here the original birch branches turn around their pinned bases, together with their crown partners; distal crowns receive rigid yaw/translation rather than radius multiplication. The willow's 89 canopy/stem/leaf roles share one spatial sweep toward the back and right. It has no independently authored native main-bough node: necessary same-owner wood connections must be derived from its unchanged original trunk. The original spire remains a separate vertical silhouette, not a fourth rounded cloud.

The complete owner matrices, pivots, maturity records, source identities and original material specifications are in `proposal.json`. All 23 original fixed roles, plus the spire's original four tiers, remain byte-digest identical. The other 31 owners, shoulder three, original terrain and highest hero are fixed context. No detail or light decorations are added.

Same-owner native boughs are added only when overlap is not found; original radius/span/cap rules and the 31 repair cap are retained. No 70-leaf repair or baseline pass is implied. First unresolved primary query is saved with its editable shape. A complete deformation may be exported as **HOLD-shape** for composition review even when a subsequent junction fails; incomplete deformation is saved as FAILED and cannot reach the view script.
