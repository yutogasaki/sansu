After load, the list held Blender Object entries. `read_references(..., donor_names)` consequently attempted string matching with an Object, then raised `TypeError: can only concatenate str (not "Object") to str` at donor_reference.py:50 / source-study.py:165.

This is a Python/Blender data-loading boundary defect in the measurement script. It is separate from run01's original-floor translation loss and provides no evidence that the source shape is infeasible. The corrected matrix lifecycle was not reached and remains unverified. No candidate results were generated; do not label them as measured geometric HOLDs.
